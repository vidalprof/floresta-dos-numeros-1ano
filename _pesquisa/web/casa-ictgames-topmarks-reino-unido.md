The transfer gap appears when a learner scores well online but freezes on a paper problem.

Plan the bridge deliberately. Use one short game, then put the same number fact inside a bar model, missing-number sentence or word problem.

For example, after practising 7 and 8 times tables, ask: "A school buys 8 packs of 7 pencils. How many pencils is that, and how do you know?" This checks whether recall has become usable maths knowledge, not only screen speed.

Fluency means accuracy, efficiency and flexibility

Fast answers are only one part of mathematical fluency.

The SERP comparison showed that competitors explain fluency as accuracy, efficiency and flexibility, while this article was too focused on speed. Use Topmarks to strengthen recall, but ask learners to show more than a quick click.

After a round of Hit the Button, ask three checks. Was the answer accurate? Was the strategy efficient? Could the learner solve the same fact another way? A child who knows 8 x 7 as 56 should also connect it to 7 x 8, 56 divided by 8, double 28, or 8 x 5 plus 8 x 2.

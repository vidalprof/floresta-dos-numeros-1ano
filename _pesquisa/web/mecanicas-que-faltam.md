This Odd One Out Worksheet/Game is a great way to put your skills to the test in a fun environment. By practicing, you’ll start to work out the answers efficiently.

Welcome to Odd One Out Worksheet/Game. This game is a logic and classification game designed to help kindergarten students identify common attributes and recognize items that do not fit a specific category. Find the one that does not belong. Scroll down the page for a more detailed explanation.

The goal is to look at a group of four items and identify the “Odd One Out”—the single item that does not share the same characteristics as the other three.

How to play the Odd One Out Worksheet/Game

Tap the large indigo “Start Mission” button on the home screen to begin.

Think about what three of the items have in common.

If you are correct: The card will glow green, and the game will explain the logic (e.g., “That’s right! One of these is a vehicle!”).

If you are incorrect: The card will shake red. Look at the group again and try a different choice!

Once you find the correct answer, the game will pause for 5 seconds to let you read the explanation before automatically moving you to the next category.

This game builds foundational Categorization skills. By determining why an item is different, students practice:

Identifying color, shape, function, or habitat.

Ruling out items that fit the “rule” of the group.

Learning the names and categories of diverse objects ranging from celestial bodies to household furniture.

In school, non-verbal reasoning enables students to analyse and solve complex problems without relying upon or being limited by language abilities. Many mathematical concepts, physics problems, computer science tasks, and science problems require strong reasoning skills. Outside of the classroom, non-verbal reasoning may be described as "common sense." While a person with common sense may or may not score high on a typical IQ test, it is common sense that gets us through our days.

So, by developing their non-verbal reasoning skills, children can aid their cognitive development, which not only helps them in the classroom, but also in everyday life too.
